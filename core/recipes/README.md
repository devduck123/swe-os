A recipe is a starting architecture for a common kind of project. Guides teach one idea at a time. A recipe puts them together into something you can build this weekend.

Every recipe answers the same questions:

- What's the smallest architecture that does the job?
- Why does each piece exist?
- What does it cost at zero users, at a thousand, and at ten thousand?
- What's deliberately left out, and what would make you add it?

The lesson isn't only what to add. It's what not to add yet.
